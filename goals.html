Career goals

Where I want to end up

I want to be an embedded and firmware engineer working on hardware where the problem actually matters to me: medical devices, space and research hardware (places like NASA JPL or Johns Hopkins APL), or clean energy. What I enjoy most is the layer where code meets physics, like reading a noisy sensor, filtering it, and acting on it on time. I'd rather understand a system from the bottom up than treat the hardware as a black box.

I also have a longer-term interest in working with Japanese engineering companies, which is one reason I'm keeping up my Japanese.

How I plan to get there

My internships so far have been software and AI work. They taught me a lot, but the jobs I want need much deeper hardware skills, so most of my plan is about closing that gap.

Fall 2026First semester at Tech: programming HW/SW systems, signals, circuits. Joined the Autobots VIP research team and the Marine Robotics Club as a firmware engineer. Start the theremin and get it to a playable prototype.
Spring 2027Digital design lab, feedback control, and the physical foundations of computer engineering. Add volume control and filtering to the theremin.

Fall 2027Embedded systems design, computer architecture, controls, and DSP. Move the theremin onto an RTOS. Start senior design.
Spring 2028Finish senior design and graduate with my B.S. in Computer Engineering.
2028–2029Stay for the BS/MS program and finish a non-thesis M.S. in ECE, focused on signal processing and control.

Skills I'm working on

  • Real-time and RTOS work. My robot runs bare-metal. The theremin is where I'll learn to split work into tasks with deadlines.
  • Signal processing. I want to be comfortable with filtering and measurement in practice, not just on paper.
  • Circuits. I want to design the analog front end of a project myself instead of copying a reference design.
  • Research. Through Autobots I'm learning how to read papers and work on an open-ended problem with a team.
